# Invoicing and Taxes

## Invoice Generation

NimbusPay generates a PDF invoice at the close of every billing cycle, itemizing the base subscription fee, any usage overage, and applicable taxes. Invoices are available in the dashboard under Billing > Invoices and are also emailed to the billing contact on file.

## Tax Calculation

NimbusPay calculates applicable sales tax, VAT, or GST based on the merchant's registered business address. Merchants can update their tax registration ID (e.g. VAT number) in Settings > Tax Information; once verified, tax-exempt or reverse-charge status is applied automatically to future invoices.

## Requesting a Corrected Invoice

If a merchant believes a line item on an invoice is incorrect — for example, a usage charge that doesn't match their own transaction records, or a tax rate applied incorrectly — this is handled as a billing discrepancy review, not a payment dispute. See `disputes/billing_discrepancy_review.md` for that process.

## Invoice History

Invoices remain accessible in the dashboard for the lifetime of the account and can be exported in bulk as a CSV for accounting purposes.
