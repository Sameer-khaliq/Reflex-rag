# Dispute Evidence Submission

This document covers evidence submission specifically for chargeback disputes (see `chargeback_disputes.md`), not billing discrepancy reviews.

## Accepted Evidence Types

- Proof of delivery or service fulfillment (tracking number, signed receipt, access logs)
- Customer communication showing agreement to the charge (order confirmation, signed terms)
- Proof that a refund was already issued for the disputed amount, if applicable

## Submission Process

Evidence is submitted through the dashboard under Disputes > [Dispute ID] > Submit Evidence, as PDF or image attachments. NimbusPay compiles the submission and forwards it to the card network on the merchant's behalf.

## Deadline

Evidence must be submitted within the 7-day response window described in `chargeback_disputes.md`. Late submissions are not guaranteed to be considered by the card network and are accepted at NimbusPay's discretion only in cases of documented system outage.

## Outcome Notification

Card networks typically take 30–75 days to issue a final chargeback ruling. NimbusPay notifies the merchant by email as soon as a ruling is received; there is no way to expedite this timeline, as it is set by the card network, not NimbusPay.
