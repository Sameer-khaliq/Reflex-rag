# Billing Discrepancy Review

A billing discrepancy review is the process for when a merchant disagrees with something on their own NimbusPay invoice — for example, an overage charge that doesn't match their internal transaction count, or a tax amount that looks wrong. This is an internal review process handled directly between the merchant and NimbusPay support, and does not involve the merchant's customers or any card network.

## How to Raise a Billing Discrepancy

From the dashboard, go to Billing > Invoices, select the invoice in question, and click "Flag for Review." Include the specific line item and the reason it appears incorrect.

## Review Timeline

NimbusPay support reviews flagged invoices within 5 business days. If the review confirms an error, a corrected invoice is issued and any overcharge is applied as account credit or refunded, depending on merchant preference.

## Difference From a Chargeback Dispute

This process is entirely separate from a chargeback dispute (see `chargeback_disputes.md`), which is initiated by a merchant's *customer* through their bank against a specific customer transaction — not by the merchant against their own NimbusPay invoice. Support teams should route "dispute" or "billing complaint" queries carefully, since merchants sometimes use the word "dispute" loosely to describe an invoice disagreement even though it is not a card-network chargeback.

## Escalation

If a merchant is not satisfied with the outcome of a billing discrepancy review, they can request a secondary review from a senior billing specialist within 10 business days of the original decision.
