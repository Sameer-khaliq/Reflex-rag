# Refund Eligibility

## What Can Be Refunded

- Subscription charges can be refunded within 14 days of the charge date if the merchant has not used any billed features during that period.
- Transaction fees on a specific processed payment can be refunded if the underlying customer payment itself is refunded.
- Overage charges are refundable only if they resulted from a documented NimbusPay system error (confirmed by support), not from normal usage.

## What Cannot Be Refunded

- Charges older than 90 days are not eligible for refund under any circumstances.
- Enterprise plan annual fees are non-refundable after the first 30 days, per the standard Enterprise agreement terms.
- Wallet balance top-ups are non-refundable once added, but remain usable indefinitely.

## How to Request a Refund

Merchants request refunds from Billing > Invoices > Request Refund, or by contacting support with the invoice number. Approved refunds are issued back to the original payment method.

## Processing Time

Once a refund is approved, processing time depends on the payment method originally used. See `refund_processing_card.md` and `refund_processing_general.md` for method-specific timing.
